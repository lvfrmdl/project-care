04 / Menstrual Health

Understanding Menstrual Health

Accessible information about menstrual wellbeing, common symptoms and when to seek professional advice.

♡

Common Symptoms

Cramps, bloating, headaches, tiredness, breast tenderness and mood changes.

☼

General Self-Care

Rest, stay hydrated, eat regular meals and consider gentle warmth for cramps.

⚕

When to Seek Help

Consult a healthcare professional for severe pain, significant changes in periods or symptoms affecting everyday activities.

Seek urgent medical attention for severe sudden pelvic pain, very heavy bleeding accompanied by faintness or severe weakness.

    05 / First-Aid Library

    Basic First-Aid Information

    General information about responding to common situations while waiting for authorised assistance.

    Minor Cuts

    Wash hands, rinse the wound with clean running water and cover it with a clean dressing. Seek help for deep or persistent bleeding.

    Minor Burns

    Cool the burn under cool or lukewarm running water for 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter or creams. Seek medical help for serious burns.

    Sprains

    Stop the activity, protect the injured area and seek medical advice if pain or swelling is significant.

    Nosebleeds

    Sit down, lean forward and pinch the soft part of the nose continuously. Seek medical assistance if bleeding is heavy or persistent.

    Feeling Faint

    Help the person lie down safely and get assistance. If they do not recover quickly, or have breathing difficulties, seek emergency help.

    Choking

    Choking can be life-threatening. Contact emergency services and get a trained first aider immediately.

    Remember: This library is introductory information, not a substitute for certified first-aid training.

    07 / About CARE

    Campus healthcare should be accessible.

    Project CARE (Campus Access to Reliable Healthcare) aims to make healthcare information and support more accessible to university students through a centralised digital platform.

    Improve Accessibility

    Provide a central location for verified campus healthcare contacts and facility information.

    Promote Awareness

    Provide reliable information about symptoms, menstrual health and basic first aid.

    Support Wellbeing

    Encourage informed help-seeking and improve awareness of available resources.

    UN Sustainable Development Goal 3

    Good Health and Well-being. CARE aligns with SDG 3 through its focus on health awareness, accessibility and student wellbeing.
